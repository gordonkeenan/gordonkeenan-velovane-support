# Getting support

- **FAQ:** <https://velovane.cc/faq.html>
- **Bugs / feature requests:** open an issue using one of the templates.
- **Questions:** use [Discussions](../../discussions).
- **Billing, subscriptions, refunds or anything personal:** [support@velovane.cc](mailto:support@velovane.cc) or <https://velovane.cc/support.html>. Refunds for App Store / Google Play purchases are handled by Apple and Google.
- **Security:** see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Issues are public — never post addresses, email addresses, receipts or route files.
